Profile

A little about how I got here. The full story is on its own page.

Psychology is how I understand people. Serving them well is why I do this work.

For as long as I can remember, I have wanted to know what happens underneath behavior: why we react before we reflect, how we form opinions, what makes us change our minds. Psychology turned that curiosity into a discipline, and a turning point as a teenager turned it into a purpose: putting that understanding in service of others.

I was raised in Madrid, continued my secondary education in England, and completed it in the United States before coming to Tulane in New Orleans, where I study psychology with minors in Strategy, Leadership & Analytics and French. Moving between cultures taught me to notice how differently people see the same situation.

My experience spans a developmental research lab at Tulane, applied behavior work, clinical observation, crisis support, bilingual community mental health, and earlier roles in business and customer service in Madrid.

In this portfolio I focus on consumer behavior: how perception, identity, and culture shape what people choose, wear, and come back to, and how brands can understand their clients as people.
