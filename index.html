Profile

A little about how I got here.

The psychology behind what we choose, wear, and come back to.

I grew up in Madrid and study in New Orleans, so I’ve spent years noticing how differently people dress, shop, and decide depending on where they are.

In Tulane’s Learning & Brain Development Lab, I turn observed behavior into clean, coded data. Earlier roles in customer service and at a Madrid company taught me how service and feedback shape people’s impressions.

I want to apply that mix of research discipline and cultural fluency to consumer insight, client experience, and the design of more personal, AI-assisted shopping.

Focus areas

Three areas where I want to build my career. Open a panel to see the questions I’d explore and the experience behind each one.

Clothing as identity

Clothing is one of the first ways people tell the world who they are.

I’m interested in how people use what they wear to express identity, signal belonging, and feel confident, and how those meanings shift between cultures.

Questions I’d explore

  • How does a piece make someone feel, compared with how they think others will see them?
  • How do cultural context and self-image shape which brands people notice?

The decision behind the purchase

Every purchase is a decision, and every decision leaves a trace.

I want to understand how customers discover a brand, form preferences, and decide to return. Research gave me the habits this needs: consistent observation, careful coding, and data a team can trust.

Questions I’d explore

  • Which moments in the client experience build trust and loyalty?
  • How do service, fit, and price perception tip a purchase, in store or online?

Personal, not generic

Personalization should feel thoughtful, never intrusive.

AI now shapes how people find and try on style, from recommendations to virtual styling. I want to help those experiences reflect real behavior and earn customers’ trust.

Questions I’d explore

  • When does a recommendation feel helpful, and when does it feel like surveillance?
  • How can design build trust in AI-assisted shopping?
