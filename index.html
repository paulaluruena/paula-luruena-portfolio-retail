01 — The Lens

Where psychology
meets style.

Three areas I want to build a career in. These are interests, grounded in the research and customer-facing experience below.

I

Fashion Psychology

Clothing as identity.

How people use what they wear to express who they are, signal belonging, and feel confident, and how culture changes those meanings between Madrid, New Orleans, and beyond.

Starting pointB.S. Psychology, cross-cultural life across Spain and the U.S., and a long personal interest in fashion, design, and photography.

II

Consumer Insight

The decision behind the purchase.

How customers discover a brand, form preferences, and decide to come back. I’m especially curious about how product presentation, service, and perceived value turn a first visit into loyalty.

Starting pointBehavioral data collection and coding at Tulane, plus customer service and team operations at 100 Montaditos.

III

AI & Design

Technology that still feels personal.

AI is changing how people find and try on style, from recommendations to virtual styling. I want to help make sure those experiences reflect real human behavior, build trust, and feel personal rather than generic.

Starting pointTulane’s AI & Society Club, where I explore human–AI interaction, bias, and privacy, plus a psychology foundation in perception and decision-making.

A closer lookQuestions I’d love to explore with a brand.
  1. Discovery

    What earns attention?

    How do visual storytelling, cultural context, and self-image influence which brands people notice?

  2. Self-expression

    What does this piece say about me?

    How do customers weigh how an item makes them feel against how they believe others will see them?

  3. Decision

    What tips the choice?

    How do service, fit, price perception, and the store environment, physical or digital, shape a purchase?

  4. Personalization

    When does AI feel helpful?

    At what point do recommendations feel thoughtful, and when do they start to feel intrusive or generic?

  5. Relationship

    What brings someone back?

    Which moments in a client’s experience build trust and a lasting relationship with a brand?
