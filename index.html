Where I want to work

Clothing is one of the first ways people tell the world who they are.

I’m interested in how people use what they wear to express identity, signal belonging, and feel confident, and in how those meanings shift between cultures. Having grown up in Madrid and studied in the U.S., I notice those differences every day.

Questions I’d explore

  • How does a piece make someone feel, compared with how they think others will see them?
  • How do cultural context and self-image shape which brands people notice?
